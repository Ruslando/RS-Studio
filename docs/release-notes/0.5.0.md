Changes since v0.3.0-alpha.

## New

- **Split view**: two spectrogram views, side by side or stacked. Each has its own active layer and playhead.
- **Compare view**: finds passages that repeat, shows two of them side by side, and pastes a transcribed passage into the others.
- **Tempo map detection**: finds beats, bars and meter changes. Notes move with a tempo marker when you drag it. Tempo and sections can differ per layer.
- **Concert pitch**: estimated on upload and shown next to A440, the default. The grid follows the pitch you set. New projects can also pitch-shift the audio.
- **Spectrogram views**: harmonic product spectrum and spectral whitening next to the CQT, reassignment for thinner lines, left, right or side channel, and intensity controls.
- **Tone presets**: DLC Builder's full gear catalogue and 23 starting presets from the official tone database. Import and export presets, and use them in tone markers.
- **Artifacts sidebar**: motifs, markers and hand anchors as references with previews. Drag them onto the stage and transpose them.
- **Preview update channel** in Settings, to try release candidates early.

## Rocksmith export

- DLC Builder's arrangement checker runs live in the editor and explains each error and warning at its note. Ignore issues per note or per category. The export stops while unresolved issues remain.
- Difficulty levels are generated as in DLC Builder, with a Dynamic difficulty switch and a report of phrases that lost levels since the last export.
- The export is a zip with the package and DLC Builder's project files: the arrangement XML, showlights, cover, audio and the .rs2dlc project, the same files DLC Builder's own import of the package makes. Choose Only the CDLC file (.psarc) to get the package alone.
- A redesigned export window: a card per layer with its own errors, warnings and notes, ignored per layer, and the arrangement as the only choice there. Tuning and capo come from the layer. Export defaults are in Settings.
- The export now includes the fretboard's hand positions and fingerings, phrase names and solo flags, crowd events, count-in ticks, a two-measure lead-in, the preview clip and audio levels, scroll speed per layer and picked bass. A Mac package and a Japanese title and artist are optional.
- Set arpeggio and handshape frames, grip-only notes, ignore, fingerless, late slide-outs and vibrato starts, and grace and bend links by hand.
- Names are checked against the game's fonts. Package details, sort names, file names and the DLC key match DLC Builder and the Toolkit.
- About 40 fixes to anchors, handshapes, chords, bends, slides, whammy, tone switches, phrases, bass tunings, average tempo and the song's end.

## Fretboard and fingering

- Hand positions are planned with the fewest shifts. They stay put once set, and you can recalculate a selection.
- Edit hand positions, chord names and fingerings on the fretboard, and move or add notes there.
- Pick a finger per note or a fingering per chord, including the thumb and barres over unused strings. On bass the hand spans five frets.
- Chords are named from their notes. Rename them on the stage or the fretboard.
- Slides into and out of a note have a reach and a length. Their landings show on the marker rail and the neck.
- Hand changes show on the marker rail. Positions and grips that don't fit or stretch too far are flagged.
- Hand positions stay on their notes when you drag notes or a reference, and every copy of a reference gets them on the same notes.
- Bends, vibrato, slides and other techniques are animated on the fretboard and played back.

## Editor

- Split notes into equal parts, toggle precise note length from the note menu, and see a warning on notes off the grid.
- Copy and paste tempo, section, phrase and tone markers. The song's end is a section marker you can drag.
- Arrow keys play the note they move to. Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down moves notes an octave; Ctrl+A selects a layer's notes.
- Bar numbers on the stage, an editable playhead time, clearer selected notes, inertial panning, and drawing settings in the spectrogram controls.
- Every layer row has a volume, with two buses. Copy and move layers. The note context menu is regrouped and searchable.
- Switching layers is faster, with a loading indicator while a spectrogram renders.
- A reference off the grid is a warning, with an auto-fix that snaps it back. Boxes less than a millisecond off snap when the project opens.
- New icons throughout.

## Projects and import

- Creating a project is simpler and reads the title and artist from the audio file.
- Guitar Pro imports keep their layers and timing, list every note problem, and show techniques as editable curves.
- A stem separated from the song remembers how it was separated. The project library has a page size setting.

## Reliability

- The desktop app quits as soon as its window closes. Edge autofill, password saving and page zoom are off. Exports no longer crash the window.
- On Linux, sound works from the first click.
- Faster drawing and fingering. Fingering pauses while you drag or while the fretboard is folded.
