Changes since v0.5.0-rc.2.

## Rocksmith export

- A new export window with tabs, export defaults in Settings and a check that stops the export on issues that are neither fixed nor ignored.
- Difficulty levels generated as DLC Builder does, with a Dynamic difficulty switch and a report of phrases that lost levels since the last export.
- Arrangement properties, phrase names and solo flags, crowd events, showlights, count-in ticks and a two-measure lead-in.
- Set arpeggio and handshape frames, grip-only notes, ignore and fingerless by hand; add late slide and vibrato starts, grace and bend links.
- Package details, sort names, file names and the DLC key follow DLC Builder and the Toolkit; optional Mac package and Japanese title and artist; names are checked against the game's fonts.
- Many corrections to handshapes, anchors, chords, bends, bass tunings, average tempo and the song's end.

## Editor and tempo

- Detect a tempo map with beats and bars, move notes along with it, and halve or double a marker's tempo.
- Estimate the concert pitch on upload, follow it in the grid and export it as a cent offset.
- Find passages similar to a range, step through matches and paste a transcribed passage into them.
- Hear notes and chords while navigating with the arrow keys; move notes an octave with Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down; select a layer with Ctrl+A.
- Bar numbers along the stage, clearer selected notes, higher grid contrast and an editable playhead time.
- Spectrogram views with stereo channel and intensity controls.

## Fretboard and fingering

- Edit hand positions, chord names and fingerings directly on the fretboard, and move or add notes there.
- Slides into and out of a note have a reach and a length; both ends of a slide are named.
- Automatic positions stay put once voiced and can be recalculated per selection.
- More natural hand movement and faster fingering, with no work while dragging or while the fretboard is folded.

## Import and reliability

- Guitar Pro imports keep their own timing and list every note problem.
- The desktop app quits right after its window closes, and Edge autofill, password saving and page zoom are turned off.
- Faster editor drawing.

This is a pre-release distributed through the Preview update channel.
